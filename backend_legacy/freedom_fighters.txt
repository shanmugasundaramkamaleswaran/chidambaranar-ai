Tiruppur Kumaran (1904–1932) was a brave Indian freedom fighter inspired by Mahatma Gandhi.
He was born as Kumaraswamy Mudaliyar in Chennimalai, Tamil Nadu.
He founded the Desa Bandhu Youth Association and led protests against British rule.
During a protest in Tiruppur, he was brutally beaten by the police near the Noyyal River.
Even while dying, he held the Indian flag tightly, earning the name “Kodi Kaatha Kumaran.”

Despite living a simple life and facing fear, pressure, and isolation, his resolve to serve the nation never weakened.
He believed sacrifice was necessary for freedom and acted with unwavering courage.
Vanchinathan overcame his struggles through strong conviction, patriotism, and fearlessness, choosing duty over his own life.




                Velu Nachiyar (1730–1796) was a courageous queen of Sivagangai who fought British rule in Tamil Nadu.
After losing her husband and kingdom, she lived in exile, facing hardship and loss.
With intelligence and alliances, she rebuilt her strength and raised an army.
She overcame her struggles through bravery, strategy, and determination, becoming one of India’s first freedom-fighting queens
